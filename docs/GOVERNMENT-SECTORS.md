# Falsum: Government AI Sector Coverage

97% of OECD countries use AI in government (2026). Falsum covers every sector where government pushes AI, because the same AI that serves citizens can surveil them. The app protects the citizen in every sector.

## Sectors Covered

### 1. Defense & Public Safety
Government uses: intelligence analysis, logistics, cyber security, surveillance, evidence review, emergency response, crime pattern analysis.
Falsum protects: 
- Module 11 (active defense): detects surveillance tools on your device
- Module 24 (mobile shield): finds stalkerware (often used for "safety" but abused)
- Module 02 (packet witness): sees what data leaves your device to government systems
- Module 12 (vault): hash-chained evidence log — if they surveil you, you have receipts

### 2. Healthcare
Government uses: medical imaging AI, disease monitoring, visit scheduling, hospital operations.
Falsum protects:
- Module 09 (broker eradicator): removes your health data from data brokers (958 brokers)
- Module 08 (breach cleaner): alerts when hospital breaches expose your data
- Module 17 (photo scrub): strips medical photo metadata before sharing

### 3. Education
Government uses: AI tutors, student monitoring, predictive analytics.
Falsum protects:
- Module 22 (tracker block): stops ed-tech trackers
- Browser extension: blocks fingerprinting on school portals
- Module 16 (mail shield): catches phishing targeting students

### 4. Tax & Revenue
Government uses: fraud detection, automated auditing, chatbots.
Falsum protects:
- Module 18 (scam shield): blocks tax-scam addresses
- Module 23 (fraud pack): builds evidence packs for tax fraud
- Vault: proves what you filed and when

### 5. Transportation
Government uses: traffic AI, license plate readers, transit optimization.
Falsum protects:
- Android app: VpnFilterService blocks location trackers
- Module 19 (wifi/router): secures your home network from IMSI catchers

### 6. Judiciary
Government uses: risk assessment, document AI, translation.
Falsum protects:
- Module 12 (vault): tamper-evident evidence log for legal defense
- Module 01 (evidence sweep): preserves digital evidence correctly

### 7. Citizen Services
Government uses: chatbots, benefit eligibility AI, document processing.
Falsum protects:
- Local AI (not cloud): your benefit questions stay on your device
- No data leaves: Falsum never sends your data to any server

## The Principle

Every sector where government uses AI to serve citizens is a sector where that AI can be turned against citizens. Falsum sits on the citizen's device, watching the watchers, keeping receipts. The government has AI. Now you do too — free, local, and yours.
